# AI Job Red Flag Detector

An AI-powered tool that analyzes job descriptions and company information to identify potential risks before applying.

## Features

- Calculates an Overall Risk Score (0-100)
- Detects unrealistic job requirements
- Identifies toxic workplace signals
- Evaluates remote job authenticity
- Highlights hiring process risks
- Assesses company stability concerns
- Lists positive signals
- Generates smart interview questions

## Analysis Categories

### Requirements Analysis
- Excessive experience expectations
- Overloaded skill requirements
- Contradictory responsibilities

### Workplace Culture Analysis
- Burnout indicators
- Hustle culture signals
- Work-life balance concerns

### Remote Work Verification
- Hidden relocation requirements
- Misleading remote claims
- Office attendance expectations

### Hiring Risk Assessment
- Missing salary information
- Vague responsibilities
- Excessive qualification demands

### Company Risk Evaluation
- Startup stability concerns
- Growth-related risks
- Reputation indicators

## Output Generated

- Overall Risk Score
- Top Red Flags
- Positive Signals
- Risk Breakdown by Category
- Final Verdict
- Smart Interview Questions

## Use Case

This project helps job seekers make informed career decisions by identifying potential warning signs in job postings and company profiles before applying.

## Tech Stack

- AI Prompt Engineering
- Natural Language Processing
- Risk Scoring Framework
- Infographic-Based Reporting

## Goal

To help candidates avoid misleading opportunities and make smarter career decisions through AI-powered job analysis.
