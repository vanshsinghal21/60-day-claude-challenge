# 🦈 AI Shark Tank Simulator

An interactive AI-powered Shark Tank Simulator that allows users to pitch their startup ideas, answer investor questions, receive intelligent feedback, get scored across multiple business metrics, and receive realistic investment decisions.

## 🚀 Features

- Modern Shark Tank inspired UI
- Startup Pitch Submission
- Four AI Investor Personas
  - Venture Capitalist
  - Founder
  - Customer
  - Angel Investor
- Interactive Pitch Round
- Dynamic AI Questions & Responses
- Startup Evaluation Engine
- Business Scoring Dashboard
- Investment Decision Generator
- Suggested Valuation & Funding
- Leaderboard System
- Downloadable Pitch Report
- Share Results
- Confetti Celebration
- Fully Responsive Design

## 📊 Evaluation Criteria

- Market Potential
- Innovation
- Business Model
- Execution
- Investment Worthiness

## 💼 Investment Outcomes

- ✅ Invest
- ❌ Reject
- 🤝 Acquire
- ⏳ Come Back Later

## 🛠️ Technologies

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Responsive UI Design
- Local Storage
- jsPDF
- Canvas Confetti

## 🎯 Purpose

The project demonstrates how AI can simulate investor feedback and help entrepreneurs validate startup ideas before presenting them to real investors.
