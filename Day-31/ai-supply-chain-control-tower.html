How This Works

Objective: Keep KPIs high and Score as high as possible before time expires.

  • Alerts appear on the left with a priority level (Critical / Medium / Low) and a countdown.
  • Pick an action before the alert's timer runs out. Each alert has one or two strong responses and several weaker or wrong ones.

  • Some effects are delayed — a decision may look fine now but cost you (or save you) a few seconds later.
  • As the shift goes on, alerts arrive faster and overlap — triage matters.
