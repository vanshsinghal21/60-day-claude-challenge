# 🏥 Prior Authorization Story Simulator

An interactive healthcare education simulator that explains the complete Prior Authorization (PA) workflow through a story-driven learning experience. Instead of reading long documentation, users follow a patient journey and make decisions at every stage to understand how healthcare providers, insurance companies, and patients interact during the authorization process.

The simulator is designed for beginners and presents complex healthcare operations in a simple, visual, and engaging way using a chat-based interface.

## 🌟 Overview

The story follows Rahul, a patient diagnosed with Rheumatoid Arthritis, and Priya, a Healthcare Operations Specialist, as they navigate the Prior Authorization process from diagnosis to final approval. Users progress through eight interactive scenes while learning how insurance reviews work, why documentation matters, how appeals are handled, and how healthcare organizations monitor authorization performance.

> **Note:** StarCare Health is used throughout the simulator as an illustrative example for educational purposes only.

## 📚 Story Flow

1. Doctor Visit
   - Diagnosis of Rheumatoid Arthritis
   - Humira prescribed

2. Insurance Roadblock
   - Provider submits PA request
   - Provider → PA Request → Payer workflow

3. What is Prior Authorization?
   - Simple explanation of PA
   - Importance of medical necessity
   - Step therapy overview
   - Educational healthcare statistics

4. Insurance Review
   - Eligibility verification
   - Clinical documentation review
   - ICD-10 diagnosis matching
   - Step therapy history validation

5. Denial
   - Missing documentation scenario
   - Understanding why denials happen
   - Operational impact on healthcare providers

6. Appeal
   - Gathering medical records
   - Letter of Medical Necessity
   - Formal appeal submission

7. Approval
   - Prior Authorization approved
   - Reference number generated
   - Permanent approval record

8. Final Takeaways
   - Patient learning summary
   - Healthcare operations metrics
   - Understanding denial rate, appeal rate, approval rate, and resolution time
