# Day 07 – Claude AI Challenge

## Topic: Claude Usage Strategy

Today I explored how to use Claude models more effectively by understanding which model and effort level works best for different types of tasks.

### What I Learned

- Claude Sonnet is the best all-round model for daily tasks such as coding, learning, and career preparation.
- Claude Haiku is useful for quick answers, summaries, and simple queries.
- Claude Opus performs best for deep research, complex reasoning, and large projects.
- Standard effort is suitable for most everyday work.
- High and Max effort should be used only when deeper analysis is required.
- Better prompts and clearer context lead to higher quality outputs.

### Key Insight

> Choosing the right model and effort level can significantly improve productivity, learning efficiency, and output quality.

### Final Recommendation

**Model:** Claude Sonnet  
**Effort Level:** Standard

This combination provides the best balance of speed, accuracy, coding assistance, learning support, and career preparation for most students.
