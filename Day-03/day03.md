# Day 3 - Role Based Prompting

## Challenge

ABTalks 60 Days Claude AI Mastery Challenge

## Topic

Role Based Prompting

## Objective

To understand how assigning different roles to AI changes the quality, depth, and perspective of the responses.

---

## Experiment

I asked Claude the same question multiple times:

> How can I build a successful AI startup in India by 2026?

The only thing I changed was the role assigned to the AI.

---

## Prompt 1: Simple Prompt

### Prompt

```
How can I build a successful AI startup in India by 2026?
```

### Output Summary

* General startup advice
* Broad market overview
* Basic suggestions on funding and product development
* Suitable for beginners

### Learning

Without a role, AI provides useful information but the response remains generic and lacks a specific perspective.

---

## Prompt 2: Role Based Prompt

### Prompt

```
You are an AI startup mentor.

How can I build a successful AI startup in India by 2026?
```

### Output Summary

* More structured response
* Better recommendations
* Clearer action steps
* More relevant insights

### Learning

Assigning a role helps AI focus on a specific domain and produce more actionable responses.

---

## Prompt 3: Founder Persona

### Prompt

```
Act as a successful AI startup founder with 10+ years of experience.

How can I build a successful AI startup in India by 2026?
```

### Output Summary

* Focus on market opportunities
* Product-market fit strategies
* Fundraising guidance
* Customer acquisition
* Business scaling roadmap

### Learning

The Founder Persona thinks from a business perspective and prioritizes growth, revenue, and market execution.

---

## Prompt 4: Developer Persona

### Prompt

```
Act as a senior AI software engineer building AI startups.

How can I build a successful AI startup in India by 2026?
```

### Output Summary

* AI architecture planning
* Model selection
* Data pipelines
* Infrastructure decisions
* Deployment and scalability

### Learning

The Developer Persona focuses on technical implementation and building reliable AI products.

---

## Key Comparison

| Prompt Type       | Main Focus                 |
| ----------------- | -------------------------- |
| Simple Prompt     | General Information        |
| Role Based Prompt | Structured Guidance        |
| Founder Persona   | Business Growth & Strategy |
| Developer Persona | Technical Execution        |

---

## Key Takeaways

* The same question can generate very different answers.
* AI performs better when given context and a role.
* Founder personas focus on business outcomes.
* Developer personas focus on technical execution.
* Role Based Prompting is one of the simplest ways to improve AI responses.

---

## Conclusion

Today I learned that AI does not only respond to the question being asked. It also responds to the role it is assigned.

A well-defined role provides context, direction, and expertise which leads to more relevant and actionable outputs.

Role Based Prompting transforms AI from a general assistant into a specialized expert.
