# Day 6/60 – Resume Optimization Using AI

## Overview

Today I worked on optimizing my resume and learning how Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) evaluate candidate profiles before they reach recruiters.

I compared my original resume with an ATS-optimized version and observed how changes in structure content and formatting can significantly improve visibility.

---

## ATS Score Improvement

📊 Before: 42/100

📈 After: 87/100

---

## Key Improvements Made

- Added a professional summary
- Improved ATS keyword optimization
- Enhanced project descriptions with stronger action verbs
- Organized resume sections more effectively
- Removed repetitive and unnecessary content
- Improved overall readability and recruiter experience

---

## Key Takeaway

A strong resume is not just about listing skills and projects.

It should be structured in a way that both recruiters and ATS systems can easily understand.

Small improvements in formatting keyword placement and content organization can make a significant difference in the overall impact of a resume.
