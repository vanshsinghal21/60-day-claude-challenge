# 📈 AI-Powered Stock Fundamental Research Skill

An AI-powered Stock Fundamental Research Skill built with Claude that helps analyze listed companies using key financial metrics, business quality indicators, valuation ratios, ownership trends, and risk factors.

## 🚀 Features

- Company Fundamentals Analysis
- Financial Health Assessment
- Revenue & Profit Growth Tracking
- Valuation Metrics Evaluation
- Ownership & Shareholding Trends
- Competitive Advantage Analysis
- Risk Factor Identification
- Industry Outlook Review
- Peer Comparison Framework
- Investor-Friendly Research Summary

## 📊 Case Study: Microsoft

To test the skill, I performed a detailed fundamental analysis of Microsoft and explored important business and financial metrics.

### Key Findings

- Strong revenue growth supported by cloud and AI businesses
- Healthy profitability with industry-leading margins
- Consistent cash flow generation
- Strong balance sheet with manageable debt
- Leadership position in cloud computing and enterprise software
