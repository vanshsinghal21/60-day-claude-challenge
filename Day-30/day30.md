# 🚚 Supply Chain Builder

**Supply Chain Builder** is an interactive business simulation that teaches the fundamentals of supply chain management through real-world decision making. Designed for students, beginners, and aspiring operations professionals, the application transforms complex supply chain concepts into an engaging, step-by-step learning experience.

Instead of presenting static theory, the simulator generates a unique company for every session and guides users through the process of building an efficient supply chain. Before every decision, the application explains what the concept means, why it matters, and how it influences business performance. Users then make strategic choices and instantly observe how those decisions impact key operational metrics.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary goal of this project is to make supply chain management easy to understand through interactive learning. Every decision demonstrates the balance between cost, speed, risk, customer satisfaction, and sustainability, helping users think like real supply chain managers.

## ✨ Features

* 🎲 Random company generation for a unique experience every playthrough
* 📚 Beginner-friendly explanations before every business decision
* 🏭 Supplier strategy selection (Single Supplier vs Multiple Suppliers)
* 🌍 Factory location planning
* 📦 Warehouse strategy optimization
* 🚛 Transportation planning across Road, Rail, Sea, and Air
* 📊 Inventory management strategy selection
* 📈 Live business dashboard with continuously updating KPIs
* 💰 Cost analysis
* ⚡ Delivery speed tracking
* ⚠️ Risk assessment
* 😊 Customer satisfaction monitoring
* 🌱 Sustainability scoring
* 🏆 Overall Supply Chain Performance Score (0–100)
* 🔍 Automatic analysis of strengths, weaknesses, biggest operational risk, and practical improvement recommendations
* 🌙 Premium enterprise-inspired dark interface
* ✨ Smooth animations, hover effects, rounded cards, and responsive design
* 🔄 Replay functionality with new randomized business scenarios

## 🧠 Learning Outcomes

This simulator helps users understand:

* How supplier selection affects business resilience
* Why factory location influences cost and delivery performance
* The role of warehouses in modern logistics
* Trade-offs between different transportation methods
* Inventory optimization strategies
* Balancing operational efficiency with customer expectations
* Building resilient and sustainable supply chains
* Making data-driven business decisions using real-world metrics

## 🚀 Why This Project?

Supply chain management powers nearly every product we use, yet it is often difficult for beginners to understand because of its complexity. This project bridges that gap by combining education, simulation, and interactive visualization into a single application.

Rather than memorizing definitions, users learn by making decisions, observing outcomes, and improving their strategies over multiple playthroughs. The result is a practical, engaging learning tool that demonstrates how operational decisions directly impact business success.
