# Day 18/60 - Claude AI Challenge

📊 Meeting Intelligence Dashboard

A dashboard designed to transform meeting transcripts and brainstorming sessions into structured insights that support faster and more informed decision making.

✨ Features
• Meeting summary generation
• Key takeaway extraction
• Action item tracking
• Open question identification
• Risk and blocker analysis
• Conflict detection
• Next steps visualization
• Modern dark themed dashboard UI

🎯 Objective
Convert unstructured meeting discussions into clear actionable insights that improve collaboration planning and execution.

🛠 Technologies & Concepts
• AI Assisted Information Processing
• Dashboard Design
• Data Visualization
• Business Intelligence
• UX Design
• Structured Decision Support
