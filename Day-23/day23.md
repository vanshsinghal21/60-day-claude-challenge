# Day 23 - Customer & MVP Blueprint

## Overview

As part of the 60 Day Claude AI Challenge, this project focuses on validating a startup idea through customer research and MVP planning.

The exercise demonstrates how a startup can move from an idea stage to a structured product strategy by identifying customer pain points, defining target users, and prioritizing features for an MVP launch.

## Topics Covered

* Executive Summary Creation
* Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
* Buyer Persona Development
* Customer Pain Point Analysis
* Customer Journey Mapping
* Buying Trigger Identification
* Customer Objection Analysis
* MVP Recommendation Framework
* MoSCoW Prioritization
* Pricing Hypothesis
* Risk Assessment
* Product Market Fit Evaluation
* Founder Action Planning

## Key Learnings

* Customer problems should drive product decisions.
* A focused MVP creates faster validation.
* Feature prioritization reduces development waste.
* Customer research improves product market fit.
* Strong startups solve urgent and recurring problems.

## Outcome

Created a complete Customer & MVP Blueprint for an AI-powered career guidance startup and evaluated its market potential through structured product management frameworks.
