# 🌍 Personal Environmental Health Analyzer

## 📖 Overview

The **Personal Environmental Health Analyzer** is an AI-powered interactive dashboard that transforms environmental data into meaningful health insights. The project combines air quality and water quality metrics to help users understand how their surroundings may impact their daily life and overall well-being.

Built using **Claude AI**, modern web technologies, and data visualization techniques, the dashboard provides an intuitive way to explore environmental conditions and their potential effects on health.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze environmental data from multiple cities
* Evaluate air and water quality conditions
* Generate an Environmental Health Score
* Visualize pollution metrics through interactive charts
* Assess potential health risks
* Provide personalized recommendations based on environmental conditions
