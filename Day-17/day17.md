🚗 Vehicle Cost & Fuel Intelligence Dashboard

An AI-powered interactive dashboard built using Claude AI to analyze vehicle ownership costs fuel efficiency maintenance expenses and environmental impact.

The dashboard transforms raw fuel and vehicle datasets into meaningful insights through visual analytics and comparison tools.

✨ Features

• Fuel cost per kilometre analysis
• Monthly running cost estimation
• CO₂ emissions comparison across fuel types
• Vehicle age vs maintenance cost insights
• Fuel efficiency trend analysis
• E85 economics and break-even calculations
• Interactive KPI cards
• Fuel comparison panels with pros and cons
• Responsive dashboard design

📊 Key Insights

• EV offers the lowest running cost per kilometre
• CNG provides strong fuel economy for daily commuting
• Diesel performs well for high mileage driving
• E85 requires balancing fuel savings against mileage impact
• Vehicle age significantly affects maintenance expenses
