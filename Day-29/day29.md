🚀 Day 29/60 – Claude AI Challenge

Today I built **Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab** which is an interactive simulation that puts the user in charge of a company during a supply chain emergency.

Instead of simply reading about supply chain management users experience it through realistic business decisions. Every choice changes important business metrics like inventory cost delivery speed customer satisfaction risk management and overall business performance.

The simulation takes the player through an entire crisis journey.

🔹 Company briefing to understand the business  
🔹 A sudden supplier bankruptcy crisis  
🔹 War Room where critical decisions must be made  
🔹 Multi-round supplier negotiations  
🔹 AI strategy recommendations  
🔹 Final performance dashboard with detailed analysis and personalized feedback

I wanted to make complex supply chain concepts easier to understand by turning them into an engaging simulation. The project focuses on learning through decision making rather than passive reading. It also challenged me to design a clean user interface while keeping the experience interactive from start to finish.
